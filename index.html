Interdisciplinary Studies in English Literature & Language

With over 15 years of experience in teaching, research, academic leadership, and student development, my academic journey has been driven by a deep interest in understanding the intersections of literature, language, cognition, psychology, culture, and technology. I hold a Ph.D. in interdisciplinary studies in English Literature and Language from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee.

My research interests span Digital Humanities, Cognitive Psychology, Psycholinguistics, Computational Text Analysis, and Literary and Cultural Studies. I am particularly interested in exploring how emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence, natural language processing, text mining, social network analysis, and digital storytelling, can open new avenues for humanities and social science research.

My current work focuses on integrating computational methods with humanistic inquiry to investigate literary texts, cultural narratives, social behaviour, and psychological phenomena, while exploring innovative approaches to cultural heritage preservation and digital scholarship.

At IIIT-NR, I have contributed to teaching, curriculum development, research supervision, and institutional leadership. I teach courses in Digital Humanities, Communication and Discourse Strategies, Cognition, Language and Speech Processing, International Language Competence, and Technical Writing. I also lead the institute’s Mental Well-Being and Digital Intervention Cell and have served in several academic and administrative capacities, contributing to student welfare, faculty development, and institutional initiatives.

My research has resulted in publications in scholarly journals, edited books, and conference proceedings across literature, psychology, communication, gender studies, and interdisciplinary research. I have supervised doctoral scholars and student research projects in areas such as computational humanities, generative AI, cultural heritage digitisation, text mining, and the computational analysis of social and psychological phenomena. I have also led a sponsored research project on resilience strategies adopted by MSMEs in Chhattisgarh. My academic contributions have been recognised through distinctions including a Best Paper Award at ICCPE 2024.

I am committed to fostering interdisciplinary collaboration between the humanities, social sciences, and emerging technologies. Through my teaching, research, and academic leadership, I seek to create opportunities for innovative scholarship, meaningful knowledge exchange, and socially relevant research that bridges humanistic perspectives with technological advancement.
