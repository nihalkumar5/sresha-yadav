RESEARCH

Digital Humanities

ELT • Cognitive Psychology

I am an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Humanities at the International Institute of Information Technology, Naya Raipur (IIIT-NR). My work brings together cognitive psychology, digital humanities, language, and literature to understand how people read, learn, communicate, and interact with technology.

I earned my Ph.D. from IIT Roorkee in 2017, with research focused on Indian English literature and cognitive reading frameworks. At IIIT-NR, I also serve as Lab-in-Charge of the Language Laboratory, working on digital language learning and communicative training.

My research explores eye-tracking and reading cognition, examining how typography, digital formats, and visual presentation influence reading behaviour and efficiency. My work in pedagogical technology has also resulted in an Indian Patent (No. 473301) for a pedagogical training tool and methodology.

Beyond cognitive psychology, my scholarship spans gender studies, ecocritical literature, and work behaviour, with interests ranging from Himalayan literature and Rabindranath Tagore's fiction to human communication and educational technology. I use experimental, psychometric, and qualitative approaches to investigate how people learn, communicate, and engage with digital environments.

Explore my publications, projects, and teaching to learn more about my research and academic work.

— Sresha
